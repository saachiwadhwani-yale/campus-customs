# Design

## Direction

I was going for slightly modernized Y2K-inspired internet. I gave the inspiration of "glossy teen magazine meets the Apple website". Y2K is very popular right now, so I thought it would be a good way to keep customers engaged, especially students, when purchasing gear from this website. Also, students' parents would probably feel a sense of nostalgia when seeing the Y2K inspired design, hopefully encouraging them to also purchase more gear.
